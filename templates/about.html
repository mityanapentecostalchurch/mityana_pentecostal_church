About Mityana Pentecostal Church

Mityana Pentecostal Church (MPC) is a vibrant Pentecostal community committed to worship, discipleship, evangelism, prayer, and transformation through Jesus Christ.

We exist to help people know God, grow in faith, serve others, and impact communities.

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ and transform lives through biblical teaching, prayer, worship, and community outreach.

Our Vision

A growing Christ-centered family impacting Uganda and beyond with the Gospel.

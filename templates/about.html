About Mityana Pentecostal Church

Mityana Pentecostal Church (MPC) is a vibrant Pentecostal Churches of Uganda (PCU) congregation committed to worship, discipleship, evangelism, prayer, and community transformation through Jesus Christ.

Mityana Pentecostal Church was founded in 1991 through the ministry of Pastor Robert Kayihura as a result of the proclamation of the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Following Pastor Robert Kayihura's relocation to Rwanda, Bishop David Rubahinda assumed leadership in 1994 and continues to provide spiritual oversight to the church.

For more than three decades, the church has remained committed to preaching the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening families, developing leaders, and impacting communities through the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

Church Motto

"A Living Church for a Healthy Community"

Our Vision

To create lasting spiritual impact in the lives of people in our community through the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

To reach, disciple, equip, and nurture people through the preaching of God's Word, prayer, worship, fellowship, and community outreach, leading them into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ and empowering them to positively impact their families, communities, and the world.

Our Goals

  • Evangelize the Community through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
  • Make Mature Disciples who know, obey, and follow Christ.
  • Promote Spirit-Filled Living through the power of the Holy Spirit.
  • Strengthen Prayer and Worship in both personal and corporate settings.
  • Build Strong Christian Families founded on Biblical principles.
  • Develop Christian Leaders for ministry and service.
  • Serve the Community through compassion and practical outreach.
  • Support Local and Global Missions to advance God's Kingdom.

Core Values

  • Biblical Authority – God's Word is our foundation for faith and practice.
  • Prayer – We depend on God through personal and corporate prayer.
  • Holy Spirit Empowerment – We value the ministry and gifts of the Holy Spirit.
  • Worship – We honor God through passionate and Spirit-led worship.
  • Evangelism – We are committed to sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ.
  • Discipleship – We help believers grow in Christlikeness.
  • Love and Compassion – We demonstrate Christ's love through service.
  • Integrity – We pursue honesty, holiness, and godly character.
  • Family – We value strong Christian families.
  • Excellence – We seek to honor God by giving our best in ministry.

Church Theme: Reaching People, Building Disciples, Transforming Communities through the Power of the Holy Spirit.
